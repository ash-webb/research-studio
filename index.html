Safeguards in this starter

  • Participants can submit records but cannot read research results.
  • Researcher access is protected by authentication and row-level authorization.
  • Tracking excludes keystrokes, input values, URL queries, and visible text by default.
  • Tree-test path scoring is independent from self-report; A/B testing preserves assignment and unscored outcomes; first-click tests can remain unscored if no target is configured.
  • Unmoderated research methods capture grouping and navigational choices but do not infer participant intent.
  • Each task and survey answer retains its session ID and any A/B assignment for analysis.
Not approved for sensitive research by default.
For federal or other regulated studies, obtain the appropriate security/privacy approvals, hosting authorization, consent language, data-retention controls, and threat review before collecting participants' data.
