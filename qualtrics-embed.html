Run a study inside a Qualtrics survey

The survey shows Research Studio on one of its pages and stores the results in its own data: the design each person saw, task outcomes, answers to the study's questions, and the full click-by-click record. Research Studio needs no database for this. Full guide

1. Declare the fields in Survey Flow

Qualtrics only keeps embedded data that is declared. Near the top of Survey Flow, add an Embedded Data element with each of these names and no values. Which names your account needs depends on the survey layout: your JavaScript test showed whether __js_rs_small or rs_small held the value. When unsure, declare both sets.

If your randomizer sets the design, it should set rs_variant to one of: . Leave rs_variant blank to let Research Studio pick at random.

2. Add the page and its JavaScript

  1. Put consent, the Paperwork Reduction Act notice and any screening on the pages before. In the study builder, tick Consent is collected in the survey so participants aren't asked twice.
  2. Add a page with one Text / Graphic question, for example “Please complete the activity.”
  3. Open that question's JavaScript and replace everything with the script below.
  4. Add your follow-up questions on the pages after it.

3. Check it in Preview

  1. Preview the survey and complete the activity with made-up answers.
  2. In Data & Analysis, include survey previews, and check that rs_embed_ok is yes and rs_data_parts is filled in.
  3. Export the responses as CSV and use Results ▸ Import Qualtrics results in Research Studio. The import checks every response's data against its checksum. Previews are left out of the results, and a note says how many were left out.

Only addresses on the list in settings.js (embedHosts, by default the Qualtrics domains) can receive results. A page that is opened anywhere else gets nothing.
